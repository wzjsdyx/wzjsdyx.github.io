# 原文

> The NIC-400 Lite is an entry-level AXI interconnect built on NIC-400 technology. It provides the advantages of the NIC-400 at a reduced cost-point. The NIC-400 Lite is aimed at low end embedded, cost-sensitive devices.

{% asset_img image-20261110194253352.png %}

![image-20261110194253352](D:\blog\wzjsdyx.github.io\source\_posts\2026-11-10-培训笔记-2-Overview-NIC-400-Lite-Overview\image-20261110194253352.png)



NIC-400 Lite is 20% faster and 20% smaller than NIC-301 and has the addition of AMBA AXI4 interfaces and interconnect switches and provides the following features:

- Long burst support, QoS signaling
- Superset of NIC-301 features
- Hierarchical clock gating
- 90% reduction in idle and near idle power on LP.
